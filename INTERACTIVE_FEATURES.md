# 🎉 Class 1 Learning Buddy - Enhanced with Interactive Animations!

## 🚀 What's New?

Your Class 1 Learning Buddy has been supercharged with **Three.js**, **GSAP**, and interactive animations to make learning more fun and engaging for kids!

---

## ✨ New Interactive Features

### 🌟 1. **3D Interactive Mascot** (Three.js)
A cute 3D star character that kids can interact with!

**Features:**
- 🎯 **Mouse Tracking**: The mascot follows your mouse cursor
- 🖱️ **Click to Bounce**: Click the mascot to make it bounce
- 🔄 **Auto Rotation**: Gentle floating and rotating animation
- 👀 **Cute Face**: Eyes, smile, and blush cheeks
- ⭐ **Floating Particles**: Stars floating around the mascot
- 🎨 **Colorful Lighting**: Dynamic pink and teal lights

**How to Use:**
1. Move your mouse over the mascot
2. Watch it follow your cursor
3. Click to make it bounce!
4. Enjoy the floating star particles

---

### 🎨 2. **Particle Background** (GSAP)
Interactive particles that respond to mouse movement!

**Features:**
- 🎈 **30 Colorful Particles**: Floating in the background
- 🖱️ **Mouse Repulsion**: Particles move away from cursor
- 🌈 **Multiple Colors**: Red, yellow, blue, pink, purple
- 💫 **Smooth Physics**: Realistic movement with damping
- 🔄 **Boundary Bouncing**: Particles bounce off edges

**How it Works:**
- Particles float around the screen
- Move your mouse to push them away
- They slowly return to their paths
- Creates a magical, interactive atmosphere

---

### ✨ 3. **Magic Cursor Trail** (GSAP)
A beautiful cursor trail that follows your mouse!

**Features:**
- 🌈 **8 Gradient Dots**: Following your cursor
- 💜 **Pink to Purple Gradient**: Beautiful color scheme
- 🎯 **Smooth Following**: Each dot follows the previous one
- 🖱️ **Click Effect**: Ripple animation on click
- 📱 **Desktop Only**: Disabled on mobile for performance

**How to Use:**
- Just move your mouse around!
- Click anywhere to see a ripple effect
- The trail creates a magical feeling

---

### 🎈 4. **Floating Shapes** (GSAP)
Emoji shapes floating in the background!

**Features:**
- 🎈 **15 Floating Emojis**: Stars, books, pencils, etc.
- 🔄 **Continuous Animation**: Float up and down
- 🎨 **Random Sizes**: Different sizes for variety
- 💫 **Rotation**: Gentle spinning motion
- 🌟 **Semi-transparent**: Doesn't distract from content

**Emojis Used:**
⭐ 🌟 ✨ 💫 🎈 🎨 📚 ✏️ 🔢 🎵

---

### 🎴 5. **Interactive 3D Cards** (GSAP)
Cards that respond to mouse movement with 3D tilt!

**Features:**
- 🎯 **Mouse Tracking**: Cards tilt toward cursor
- 📐 **3D Perspective**: Realistic 3D rotation
- 🎈 **Hover Scale**: Cards grow on hover
- 💥 **Click Bounce**: Elastic bounce on click
- ✨ **Sparkle Effect**: Sparkles appear on click
- 🌈 **Dynamic Shadow**: Shadow changes with tilt

**How to Use:**
- Move mouse over cards to see 3D tilt
- Hover to see them grow
- Click for bounce and sparkle effects

---

### 📄 6. **Page Transitions** (GSAP)
Smooth, animated page transitions!

**Features:**
- 🎬 **Fade & Slide**: Pages fade in and slide up
- 📊 **Stagger Animation**: Children animate one by one
- 🎯 **Smooth Easing**: Professional animation curves
- ⚡ **Fast & Responsive**: 0.5s transition time

**Animation Sequence:**
1. Container fades in and slides up
2. Child elements animate with stagger
3. Smooth elastic easing for bounce effect

---

### 🔢 7. **Interactive Number Line** (GSAP)
A clickable number line for maths learning!

**Features:**
- 🎯 **Clickable Numbers**: Click to select
- 🎨 **Visual Feedback**: Numbers bounce and change color
- 🧮 **Auto Sum**: Calculates sum of selected numbers
- 💫 **Elastic Animation**: Bouncy number selection
- 📊 **Progressive Display**: Shows selected numbers

**How to Use:**
1. Click numbers to select them
2. Watch them bounce and turn purple
3. See the sum update automatically
4. Click again to deselect

---

### 📊 8. **Animated Progress Bars** (GSAP)
Beautiful animated progress indicators!

**Features:**
- 🎬 **Smooth Animation**: Bars animate from 0 to value
- 🌈 **Gradient Colors**: Purple to pink gradient
- ✨ **Shimmer Effect**: Moving light effect
- 📊 **Percentage Display**: Shows progress percentage
- 🎯 **Customizable**: Easy to change colors and values

│   ├── PageTransition.tsx         # GSAP page transitions

│   └── AnimatedProgressBar.tsx    # Animated progress
├── utils/

│   └── animations.ts              # GSAP utilities
